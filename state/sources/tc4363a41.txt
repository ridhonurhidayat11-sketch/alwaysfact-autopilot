It is often called the "Cold Moon" in reference to the frigid temperatures that prevail in the Northern Hemisphere around the time of its rising, according to the Old Farmer's Almanac . December's full moon will also occur close to perigee — the moon's closest point to Earth in its elliptical orbit — giving rise to a spectacular supermoon. A supermoon can appear 30% brighter and 14% larger than a full moon at its farthest point from Earth (also known as a micromoon).. According to the late, great astronomer Fred Espenak , the Cold Moon will be just 221,667 miles (356,740 kilometers) from Earth when it turns full, making it the closest supermoon of 2026! When to see the December full moon The full Cold Supermoon will make for a spectacular sight when it rises over the eastern horizon at sunset on Dec. 23, mere hours before reaching maximum illumination. Remember to use Time and Date's moonrise and moonset tracker for timings specific to your location. Earth's satellite may appear larger than normal while close to the horizon.
