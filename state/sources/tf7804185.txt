Out in the Bristol Channel, nestled between Wales and England, the rugged island of Lundy is one of the UK’s most pioneering conservation stories. From the return of thousands of seabirds to thriving marine life beneath its waves, decades of conservation have helped transform this small island. But Lundy’s story isn’t simply about keeping people out. Local fishermen helped shape the marine protected area, and the UK’s first ever no-take zone was designed alongside the needs of the people who depend on these waters. Today, the island is home to thriving wildlife, a working fishing community and thousands of visitors each year. Mongabay’s Video Team wants to cover questions and topics that matter to you .
