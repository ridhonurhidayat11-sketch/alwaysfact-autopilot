Melt the butter and add the onion, green onions, and celery and lightly sauté. Remove from heat. Add the cream, thyme, salt and pepper. In a large bowl, combine crushed crackers and vegetable mix. Add the oysters to the bowl and toss lightly. Add reserved oyster liquid until stuffing is moist, about ¼ of a cup. Place in a shallow greased baking pan. Bake, uncovered, at 400 deg
