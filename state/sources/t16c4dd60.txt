The U.S. Federal Communications Commission (FCC) just granted SpaceX approval to deploy one million of its Starmind AI data-center satellites in orbit. "This action opens a bold new frontier for the computing infrastructure needed to power America's leadership and growth," FCC Chairman Brendan Carr said today (Nov. 25) in a statement announcing the approval. AI has become central to SpaceX's business plans and identity over the past year or so. For example, the company has said that it's eyeing a "total addressable market" of $28.5 trillion over the long haul — and nearly $23 trillion of that is in "enterprise AI applications." Starmind is key to those lofty ambitions. SpaceX founder and CEO Elon Musk believes that the future of AI compute is in space, where energy (from the sun ) is unlimited and there are no land-owning neighbors to lodge NIMBY complaints.
