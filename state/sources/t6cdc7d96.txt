Find out more about how we test and review products. Jump To: Design Performance Functionality Should I buy the YunLone Galaxy Projector? The YunLone Galaxy Projector is an inexpensive but effective way to bring the marvel of space into your living room or bedroom. Costing around $28, this star projector can display beautiful dancing auroras across your walls and ceiling, and thanks to app support, a huge range of color options are available. In fact, the color combinations you can display here are almost infinite. Key specs Size: 3.54 x 3.15 x 4.72 inches (9 x 8 x 12 cm) Weight: 17.6 ounces (500 g) Bulb Type: LED Laser: Yes Operation: App and remote control Rotation: Yes Sleep timer: Yes Speaker: Yes With built-in sounds, a Bluetooth speaker and endless customization options, this really is an all-singing, all-dancing star projector. We're amazed at just how much is loaded into this package for such a low price. It's surprisingly good quality, too, with the unit itself being sturdy and made out of hard-wearing plastic. This isn't a scientific star projector, of course. For that, we'd recommend the Sega Toys Homestar Classic or the Hommkiety Galaxy Projector . But even though the YunLone Galaxy Projector won't give you an accurate view of the Northern Hemisphere's night sky or a close-up of the moon, it's still a projector we recommend. If you're looking to create a relaxing atmosphere, it's one of the best star projectors we've reviewed.
