The Riemann Hypothesis has baffled mathematicians for 165 years, but mathematician Levent Alpoge believes he has found a proof, working with AI system Claude. Tom Whipple and mathematician Kit Yates explore what this could mean for mathematics and the humans who dedicate their lives to it. Plus, head to the University of Reading to discover how an Allosaurus ate its prey. With the help of a contraption called Dinosaw and a roast chicken, Manabu Sakamoto is trying to understand how the teeth of these prehistoric predators cut through flesh.
