The new report focuses on China , Russia and the emerging space powers of Iran and North Korea. It looks at what's happening in Earth orbit and beyond, including cislunar space, the region between our planet and the moon . The report — the 2026 edition of "Challenges to Security in Space" — is the third iteration of the DIA's unclassified intelligence assessment on space-related challenges and threats to U.S. national security, as well as the extent to which space is treated internationally as a warfighting domain . Information within the report, which you can read here , is current as of July 2026. DIA's mission is to provide intelligence on foreign militaries to help prevent wars, and win the ones that are fought. Up-front finding The report's up-front finding is that China, Russia, Iran, and North Korea are developing, testing, deploying and using space capabilities "in a way that threatens the security of all space systems and their associated space benefits."
