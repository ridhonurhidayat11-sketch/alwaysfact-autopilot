A Falcon 9 rocket lifted off from Vandenberg Space Force Base's Space Launch Complex-4E at TK11:08 a.m. ET (1508 GMT) , carrying the U.S. Space Force's USSF-266 mission. It was the fourth mission SpaceX has launched for the military branch this month. While details about the mission's payload weren't disclosed, it's the fifth space launch in support of U.S. national security in the last two weeks, and comes amid efforts by the Space Force to expand its communications and tracking capabilities. (Image credit: SpaceX) About two and a half minutes after liftoff, Falcon 9's first-stage booster separated from the rocket's upper stage, which continued toward low Earth orbit with the classified payload. The booster, meanwhile, began a descent toward SpaceX's Of Course I Still Love You droneship in the Pacific Ocean, where it touched down approximately eight and a half minutes after launch. Tuesday's launch marked the 29th flight for this Falcon 9 booster, B1081, which previously launched NASA 's Crew-7 astronaut mission to the International Space Station , the PACE and EarthCARE science satellites , two other classified Space Force launches (USSF-153 and USSF-481), and 16 Starlink missions.
