Facebook Linkedin Threads Whatsapp Reddit Email Page link MIDDLETOWN, Iowa (AP) — The U.S. Army says it plans to detonate its entire stockpile of lead azide in Iowa on Wednesday morning, warning that the dangerously degrading bomb-making chemicals could spread toxic lead within the area. Officials sought to assure residents Tuesday that they’ve taken steps to put barriers around the blast zone and limit the plume from the detonation. “We don’t believe that the public should be in a panic,” said Acting Secretary of the Army Adam Telle. The controlled detonation of five tons of degraded lead azide at the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant in Middletown has set off concerns about potential health and environmental effects and frustration over a lack of information.
