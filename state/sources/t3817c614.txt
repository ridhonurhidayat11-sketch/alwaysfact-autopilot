View author publications Search author on: PubMed Google Scholar Email Bluesky Facebook LinkedIn Reddit Whatsapp X Save article View saved research Biodiversity in the Himalayas is facing growing pressure from natural disasters that affect several nations, such as the avalanche and flood that occurred in Nepal this year (see C. Huggel et al . Nature 658 , 868–871; 2026 ). Events with cascading effects such as landslides, debris flows and floods destroy habitats and can also affect downstream ecosystems through sediment deposition and river-course changes, driving severe, long-term habitat fragmentation.
