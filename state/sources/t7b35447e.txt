Facebook Linkedin Threads Whatsapp Reddit Email Page link What kind of wealth do Borneo’s ancient rainforests hold? Industries such as logging and palm oil have generated vast monetary wealth for some. But for the Indigenous communities who call the Malaysian Borneo home—its forests, rivers and mountains hold a different kind of value. These forests are interconnected ecosystems that sustain communities with food, medicine and livelihoods, while holding generations of knowledge, traditions and culture. But as industrial pressures continue to pressure these landscapes, some communities are working harder than ever to defend their homes and protect the ecological wealth that exists in these forests.
