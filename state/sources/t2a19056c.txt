Video released by Nasa showed the moment of impact in 2022, while new images show the extent of the damage done to the asteroid. Dimorphos, the asteroid, posed no threat to Earth, but the crash was a test of our planetary defences to see if it was possible to knock a space rock off course. Four years on, a European Space Agency (ESA) spacecraft called Hera is scouring the scene to fully assess the aftermath of the collision. The BBC's science editor Rebecca Morelle explains.
