Photographer Francisco Bailón Rodríguez captured the scene from the Albercutz Watchtower, looking toward the Formentor Peninsula, after several days of high pressure and humid nights created the perfect conditions for fog to blanket the landscape. On Nov. 17, the photograph, titled "Mares de Niebla (Seas of Fog)," was named overall winner of the Royal Meteorological Society's competition, which attracted more than 5,500 entries from photographers around the world. The competition celebrates the ephemeral scenes created by weather, which can for brief moments transform landscapes into something extraordinary.
