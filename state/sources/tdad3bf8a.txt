What's going on? - video Sex among British adults has slumped in recent decades, with under-45s having half as many monthly encounters as in 1990 and one in three under-25s reporting that they have never had sexual intercourse. To unpick some of the possible reasons behind these declines, Madeleine Finlay hears from science editor and co-host Ian Sample. He tells her why young people might be having sex later, and what could account for the striking rise in women reporting that they don’t want children.
