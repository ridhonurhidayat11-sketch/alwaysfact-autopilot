Facebook Linkedin Threads Whatsapp Reddit Email Page link Turns out bumblebees are spontaneous problem solvers too! In a series of experiments, scientists found that untrained bumblebees were able to find novel solutions to get a reward. The research builds on previous experiments that showed that bumblebees can work as a team and possess cognitive abilities despite their tiny brains.
