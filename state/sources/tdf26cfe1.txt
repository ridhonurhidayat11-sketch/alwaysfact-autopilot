Kidlington Green councillors Ian Middleton and Fiona Mawson said residents were concerned by the number of hedgerows and trees recently cut down on Oxford Road. Adjacent fields have planning permission for 800 homes as part of wider plans for more than 4,400 to be built in the area. A spokesperson for developer Bellway said all the work had been done with an ecologist on site and complied with outline planning consent. Image caption, The trees and hedgerows were cut back on land next to the Oxford Road The cycleway would help connecting Kidlington to Oxford, running alongside the A4165, Oxford Road. The route is expected to get busier with significant growth planned for north Oxford, including a potential new stadium for Oxford United. According to Bellway, the trees and hedges were cut down between 26 October and 6 November to "create access" to the site planned for housing, as well as to "facilitate" the work for the cycleway. The proposals include "significant landscaping and ecological enhancements" as well as 50% affordable housing and "other infrastructure", it said. He also said its actions were "nonsensical" and premature, given the cycle path was not "imminent". "If all they needed was access, why have they ripped out an entire hedgerow?" he said. "We knew some of this was going to be taken out, but it's the scale of it that's really impacted people and sent shock waves across the local community." A spokesperson for Bellway said it had been working with Cherwell District Council "to agree details of a replacement wildlife corridor," including planting 150 new trees.
