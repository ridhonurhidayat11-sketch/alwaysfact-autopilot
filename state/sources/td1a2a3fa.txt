It is the first time the parade has been postponed due to weather. Fans and volunteers voiced their disappointment over the postponement, with one remarking: 'We can't control the weather!' Preparations to hold the iconic holiday celebration, known for its massive balloons that float among the city's skyscrapers, had already begun when the forecast for heavy rain and strong winds on Thursday led to the delay. It is hoped the parade will take place on Saturday instead.
