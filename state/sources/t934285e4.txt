Find out how to listen to other BBC stations Episode details Radio 4 , · 19 Nov 2026 , · 28 mins The quest to build a resuseable rocket BBC Inside Science Play Bookmark Bookmark Subscribe Subscribe Available for 33 days SpaceX has successfully caught the upper stage of its enormous Starship rocket for the first time. It's a major milestone in the company's ambition to make the entire rocket reusable. But SpaceX wants to go further, developing a system that can be turned around and launched again within hours, much like an aircraft at an airport, an ambition NASA also pursued when spaceflight began in earnest. Are SpaceX on the cusp of a galactic revolution and what could it mean for the future of the space economy if they are? Malcolm Macdonald, President of the Royal Aeronautical Society shares his thoughts. And 52 years ago, a message containing a wealth of information about life on Earth was beamed into space, in the hope that an extraterrestrial civilisation might one day receive it. Science writer Philip Ball joins Tom Whipple to describe how you pack a message into a radio wave, while Nadia Drake, daughter of the physicist Frank Drake, who helped devise it, reflects on her father's legacy and his contribution to the search for extraterrestrial intelligence.
