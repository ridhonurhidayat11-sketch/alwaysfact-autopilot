View author publications Search author on: PubMed Google Scholar Email Bluesky Facebook LinkedIn Reddit Whatsapp X Save article View saved research The preprint server arXiv introduced a new limit of two papers per month for each submitting researcher. This is a necessary emergency measure against slop generated by artificial-intelligence tools (see Nature 658 , 854; 2026 ). However, it risks becoming a blunt instrument that penalizes legitimate high-output researchers while leaving the underlying problem unsolved.
