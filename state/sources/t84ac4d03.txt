Facebook Linkedin Threads Whatsapp Reddit Email Page link Smooth-coated otters are increasingly being spotted along Kuala Lumpur’s Klang River, raising hopes that years of clean-up efforts are improving water quality and restoring urban wildlife habitats. However, their growing presence is not without conflict. Habitat loss caused by development has driven some otters into residential areas, where they have raided fish ponds, prompting calls for residents to adapt rather than harm these protected animals. Conservationists and city officials hope the otters can encourage further efforts to clean up the Klang, reduce waste entering the river and restore more natural riverbanks.
