months ago 1 min read Studying Cancer Cells in Microgravity HQ Web Team Nov 23, 2026 Image Article Encapsulate Inc. This Nov. 19, 2026, image shows gastric microtumors grown within an Encapsulates biochip. The Encapsulate-1 investigation studies patient-derived tumor samples aboard the International Space Station to better understand how cancer cells move and interact in microgravity. The results could help researchers develop further testing to predict a patient’s risk of metastasis and support more personalized cancer treatments. Orbiting more than 200 miles (320 km) above Earth, the space station is a powerhouse of cutting-edge science that is unlocking discoveries not possible on Earth. Image credit: Encapsulate Inc.
