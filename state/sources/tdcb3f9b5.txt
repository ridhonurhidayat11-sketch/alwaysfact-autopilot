Great news for gamers, but what about sci-fi fans? To this day, Rockstar Games sadly hasn't embraced sci-fi with any of its open-world games (save for those UFO Easter eggs). It doesn't seem likely they'll journey to the stars anytime soon — you can't really do cutting societal satire about space — but that doesn't mean there aren't GTA-adjacent sci-fi adventures set in outer space. The frustrating thing is that no one has exactly cracked that pitch yet. Non-linear games set in outer space tend to be either too ambitious, like No Man's Sky, or too focused on specific experiences like space combat, like Star Wars: Squadrons. With new hardware powering far more complex open-world games, it's only a matter of time before "GTA in space" is a real thing. For now, there are some space games that let us explore sci-fi worlds, roleplaying as criminals and outlaws amongst the stars.
