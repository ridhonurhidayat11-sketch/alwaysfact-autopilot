Each monthly full moon has its own set of informal names , often bestowed to reflect events occurring in the world at the time of their rising. The November moon is often known as the Beaver Moon, a name associated with the time of year when beavers prepare for winter and when trappers historically set beaver traps before waterways froze. November's full moon served as the opening salvo in a series of four dazzling supermoons , which turn full around the time that the natural satellite passes closest to Earth in its 27-day elliptical orbit. That added proximity can make it appear noticeably brighter and larger than the smallest full moon of the year, setting the stage for an incredible photo shoot as the lunar disk climbed high into the autumn sky.
