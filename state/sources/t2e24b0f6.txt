The European Space Agency's (ESA's ) Hera probe launched atop a SpaceX Falcon 9 rocket from Cape Canaveral Space Force Station on Oct. 7, 2024 . Its destination is the Didymos-Dimorphos asteroid system, which consists of the larger Didymos , a " rubble pile" asteroid , and its smaller moonlet Dimorphos. NASA slammed a kinetic impactor into Dimorphos in 2022 in the historic DART test , which confirmed that such an impact can alter the trajectory of an asteroid. Hera arrives at Didymos today, and ESA is sharing the first close-up images from the mission in a free online livestream. ESA's livestream starts at 1:45 p.m. ET (1845 GMT) on Wednesday (Nov. 25) . Watch it live here, courtesy of the European Space Agency. Revealing Hera's first images of asteroid Dimorphos - YouTube Watch On At Dimorphos, Hera will inspect the wreckage caused by DART (Double Asteroid Redirect Test), humanity's first test of planetary defense. The test was designed to see if the orbit of a threatening asteroid could be altered, taking it off a potential crash course with Earth. A simulation of the impact of DART on Didymos (Image credit: S.D. Raducan) The DART mission succeeded, shortening Dimorphos' orbit by just over 30 minutes, showing that kinetic impacts can indeed alter the trajectories of asteroids.
