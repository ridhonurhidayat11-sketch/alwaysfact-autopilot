That vehicle, known as Ship 42, launched Nov. 13 on Starship 's 15th test flight. A day later, it returned from orbit, nestling softly into the "chopstick" arms of its launch tower at SpaceX 's Starbase site in South Texas. Ship 42 made it through Flight 15 in good condition, according to SpaceX founder and CEO Elon Musk , who said it "could probably be reflown with a few weeks of refurbishment." But that's not going to happen. "In this case, because it's kind of an historic ship, we're going to, I think, not take any chances with the ship and just kind of leave it for public display, because what it's done is historic," Musk said on Wednesday (Nov. 18) during a discussion at the TomorrowX Summit . He didn't say where Ship would be put on display, but the likeliest locale is Starbase, SpaceX's Starship manufacturing and launch hub. (SpaceX plans to shift most Starship launches to its new Starbase Louisiana site, but construction has yet to begin on that $100 billion complex.)
