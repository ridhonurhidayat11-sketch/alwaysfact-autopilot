The incredible link between body odour and relationships that last The longer you have been with your partner, the more likely you are to find their natural scent arousing, according to a study from Radboud University in the Netherlands Tue 24 Nov 2026 16.32 GMT Last modified on Wed 25 Nov 2026 05.05 GMT Share Prefer the Guardian on Google Name: Body odour. Age: Everyone has always smelled of something. Function: Phwoar. This cannot possibly be right. I just sat next to a guy on a bus, and his scent was definitely not phwoar. Let me ask you a question: are you in a long-term romantic relationship with this man? No, of course not. Well, there’s your answer, then. If you were, you would have scientifically found his smell irresistible. I’m not sure about that. Do not question science, especially not the paper just published by the Behavioural Science Institute at Radboud University in the Netherlands, which claims that the longer a couple have been together, the more likely they are to find each other’s body odour sexually arousing. This doesn’t sound right. Trust me. Researchers got their subjects to fish out a T-shirt worn by their partner and sniff deeply from it, rating the smell by its pleasantness, intensity, and its soothing and sexually arousing aspects. And what did they discover? Well, although you might assume that the smell of someone you’ve been going out with for a while would primarily feel safe and familiar, it actually just makes you quite horny. So it isn’t like getting used to a bad smell? Absolutely not. The study found that odour-evoked sexual arousal grew throughout the duration of a relationship. People who had been together for 400 months were almost four times more likely to be aroused by smell than people who had just got together. This is astounding. I just have to get someone used to my smell and they’ll fall in love with me. Hang on, it isn’t quite that simple. Why is that? Because the study also found that smell wasn’t the be-all and end-all. When it comes to overall relationship satisfaction, there are more significant predictors. Such as what? Such as personality and competence. Oh no. And looks too, come to think of it. This is a disaster! When will fate finally smile upon the useless and ugly and horrible? Hey, don’t be like that. There is someone out there for everyone. Maybe working on your natural scent is a good first step. Actually, what is your natural scent? Well, I’m a chainsmoking durian fanatic who works in a fish factory and lives in a house made of burnt hair. There is lots to be said for longterm singledom too. Maybe you can love me. Come here and smell my T-shirt. Smell it! Well, this has all been rather pleasant, but I’m afraid I must be off.
