An Ariane 6 rocket carrying 36 satellites for the Amazon Leo constellation is scheduled to lift off from Europe's Spaceport in Kourou, French Guiana on Monday, during a 29-minute window that opens at 6:09 a.m. EST (1109 GMT; 8:09 a.m. local time in Kourou). You can watch it live here at Space.com, courtesy of Arianespace, the French company that operates the Ariane 6. Coverage will begin about 30 minutes before launch. An Ariane 6 rocket launches 32 Amazon Leo satellites to orbit from French Guiana on Feb. 12, 2026. (Image credit: ESA/CNES/Arianespace-ArianeGroup/Optique Video du CSG-P. Piron) Amazon Leo, so named because it will operate in low Earth orbit , will compete with SpaceX's Starlink internet constellation, which currently consists of more than 11,000 operational spacecraft. Amazon's network will eventually have more than 3,200 satellites, but just 400 or so have reached orbit to date. Another 40 were supposed to be delivered on Nov. 15, but they were lost after their rocket ride, a United Launch Alliance (ULA) Vulcan Centaur , suffered a problem shortly after launch. Jeff Bezos' company has also booked rides on ULA's Atlas V rocket, Blue Origin's New Glenn and SpaceX's Falcon 9 . Vulcan will be grounded for a while as ULA investigates the Nov. 15 failure and works to fix whatever caused it.
