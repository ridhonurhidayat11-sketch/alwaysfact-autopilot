View author publications Search author on: PubMed Google Scholar Email Bluesky Facebook LinkedIn Reddit Whatsapp X Save article View saved research ArXiv’s cap of two submissions per researcher per month is a defensible shield for the preprint platform’s voluntary moderators (see Nature 658 , 854; 2026 ). However, treating slop produced by artificial-intelligence tools as a moderation-workload issue understates the problem. As the article notes, papers turned away from one platform simply resurface on another website.
