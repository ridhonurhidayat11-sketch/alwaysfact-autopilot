Right now, lunar shipping runs about $544,000 per pound ($1.2 million per kilogram), so anything that astronauts can make on the moon using existing materials could help a lot. Scientists have already proposed using regolith for building 3D-printed bricks , solar arrays , and even as soil to grow potatoes . Now, a team of researchers from Purdue University in Indiana have developed an aerogel made from simulated regolith for insulating lunar habitats. New aerogels created by Purdue University researchers, made of cellulose and lunar regolith simulant, could pave the way for insulating materials made on the moon.
