Crowded together with the Orion spacecraft, the four astronauts that make up the Artemis III crew hold up three fingers, taking a brief pause in their rigorous training for some lighthearted crew bonding. In the snapshot , the crew — from left to right, NASA astronauts Randy Bresnick (mission commander) and Frank Rubio (mission specialist), European Space Agency astronaut Luca Parmitano (mission pilot) and NASA astronaut Andre Douglas (mission specialist) — is spotted posing alongside the same Orion spacecraft that they will fly to space as part of their mission. The four astronauts will launch to low-Earth orbit around Earth where they plan to practice rendezvous and docking with both Blue Origin and SpaceX's lunar landing vehicles , Blue Moon and Starship, respectively.
