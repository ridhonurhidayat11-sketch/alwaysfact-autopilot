That pioneer is Pakistan's Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, who arrived at Tiangong aboard the Shenzhou-24 spacecraft early this morning (Nov. 25). "The Chinese space station is always something to look forward to!" the China Manned Space Agency (CMSA) wrote in an update today (in Mandarin; translation by Google). "China's 'Tiangong' welcomes partners from all over the world to visit!" Ali, a former pilot in Pakistan's armed forces, is a payload specialist on Shenzhou-24. He found his way onto the mission thanks to a space-cooperation agreement that China signed with Pakistan in Islamabad in February 2025 . Ali and his two Shenzhou-24 crewmates — commander Tang Hongbo and flight engineer ​Wu Yi — enjoyed a short and smooth ride to their orbital destination. They lifted off atop a Long March 2F rocket on Tuesday (Nov. 24) at 7:18 p.m. EST (0018 GMT on Nov. 25) and reached Tiangong a little over six hours later.
