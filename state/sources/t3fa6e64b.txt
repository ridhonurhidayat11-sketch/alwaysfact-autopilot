Three Flagship Reports: 2025 Status of Stocks, 2024 Fisheries of the United States, and 2024 Fisheries Economics of the United States October 30, 2026 These annual reports cover different aspects of the status of United States marine fisheries, including data trends over time and impacts to our economy. Feature Story | Alaska New England/Mid-Atlantic Pacific Islands Southeast West Coast National PROVINCETOWN, MASSACHUSETTS (April 24, 2023)—MacMillan Pier in Provincetown. Credit: NOAA Fisheries. NOAA Fisheries is pleased to share three of our flagship reports: Status of Stocks 2025 Annual Report to Congress on the Status of U.S. Fisheries , 2024 Fisheries of the United States , and 2024 Fisheries Economics of the United States. Each of these reports is a crucial part of how we track and monitor the state of United States marine fisheries . 2025 Status of Stocks The 2025 Status of Stocks is an annual report to Congress on the status of U.S. fisheries. We track 522 stocks or stock complexes to determine their status: Overfished : population size is too small Subject to overfishing : harvest rate is too high Rebuilt: target level supports maximum sustainable yield In fiscal year 2025, NOAA Fisheries conducted 173 stock assessments and had two first-time stock status determinations as the result of improved or new scientific endeavors.
