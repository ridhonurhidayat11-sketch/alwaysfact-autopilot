Sign Up The history of chemistry predates science itself; for thousands of years, kings and servants relied on alchemists to concoct “magical” ointments and elixirs. That practice morphed over the years into a formal field of investigation. Chemistry is the field of science that tells us what stars are made of, what’s safe to eat and how our species will live on a changing planet in the future. Chemistry is everywhere and connects to nearly everything. As Kit Chapman says in the prologue of The Age of Alchemy: How Early Innovators Shaped Modern Chemistry : “Before the Enlightenment, there were only wizards, witches and wishful thinking,” but slowly a “new science arrived to change our world.” The scientific study of chemistry has saved lives, caused massive destruction and informed a greater understanding of who we are and what we’re made of.
