The Emergence of Dark Nebula Lynds 1251 Explanation: If you stare into space for a long time, what might you see? At first, you might only see the usual brightest stars , but if your eyes (or camera) could continue to collect light , you would see increasingly more. In the direction of the featured image , after only a few minutes, you would begin to notice some regions without stars . As your staring continued, the dark region would become better defined, and after a full 20 hours, you would notice a strange dark structure . To celebrate, you use digital processing to brighten the image and enhance the colors. At the end, you might have an excellent image of Lynds 1251 (LDN 1251), a star -forming region laced with opaque brown-appearing interstellar dust. Also known as the Rotten Fish Nebula, LDN 1251 is home not only to young stars and dust , but also, your long staring indicates, energetic shock waves , fast stellar winds , and powerfully beamed jets .
