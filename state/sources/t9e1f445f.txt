Facebook Linkedin Threads Whatsapp Reddit Email Page link NEW YORK (AP) — Going into this year’s U.N. climate talks in Turkey, expectations were notably low. Still, the outcome was even worse than expected—a failure to reach an overall agreement at a time of frequent extreme weather events fueled by climate change. While negotiations collapsed over one main point of contention—whether to include language about fossil fuels, the main driver of global warming—this fight over this has been building for many years. During previous conferences, nations frequently threatened to walk away if there wasn’t a strong agreement on fossil fuels. Instead, in the end, they voiced their disagreements in closing plenaries and ultimately got in line. This time, many countries were not willing to do that.
